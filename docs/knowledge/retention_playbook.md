# Retention offer rules

## Who gets an offer
Only customers with positive estimated uplift and positive net value. Never contact customers predicted to react badly (negative uplift), even if their churn risk is high.

## Contact cooldown
Do not contact a customer who received a retention offer in the last 30 days. Repeated offers train customers to wait for discounts and annoy the rest.

## Reviewer sign-off
A human reviewer checks every campaign list before it is sent. The agent attaches the experiment checks, the chosen estimator and why, the expected saved customers and net value, and any warnings. The reviewer can reject the list or add exclusions, which the agent stores in memory for the next run.

## Offer content
The standard offer is a 20% discount on one monthly fee plus a check-in message.
